# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, respectful and welcoming environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behaviour include:

- Being respectful to everyone.
- Using friendly and inclusive language.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping other contributors.
- Respecting different opinions.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Offensive comments.
- Personal attacks.
- Sharing inappropriate content.
- Disrespecting other contributors.

## Responsibilities

Project contributors are expected to follow these guidelines and maintain a respectful environment.

## Conclusion

Everyone is responsible for maintaining a safe, friendly and respectful project environment.
